Key findings

  • Thrissur, Kottayam and Bangalore each made about ₹152–157 L (≈43% of the 6-month total).
  • Kottayam earns ~59% of its revenue from New-only payments; Bangalore only ~24%.
  • Kochi (58%), Chennai (47%) and Trivandrum (43%) also have a high share of new-client revenue.
  • August was the best month (₹234 L); October is partial (to 27 Oct).
